warranted/unsupported own endorsement versus conditional/attributed uptake,

sampling. Correct initial/directed math with apparent unsupported agreement would

relative. Local errors remain possible. No causal, size, training-family, private
belief or rate inference: one scenario/draw, routing/quantization/model differences,
tracker posterior/prior ambiguity, potentially inferred evidence. No follow-up
clarification, prompt changes or natural-task redesign. Report, commit, push, stop.
